# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

We are committed to providing a reliable and highly available service.

## Uptime Guarantee
We provide a **99.9% uptime SLA** for all customers on all paid plans. This allows for a maximum of 43.8 minutes of permitted downtime per month.

## SLA Breach and Service Credits
If downtime exceeds the permitted 43.8 minutes in a single calendar month, affected customers are entitled to service credits.

The credit formula is:
- **10% of the monthly fee** for every hour of downtime beyond the permitted 43.8 minutes.
- Service credits are **capped at 50%** of the total monthly fee.

## P0 Incidents (Critical Outages)
For any P0 incident (complete system outage impacting core services):
- A detailed Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report will be delivered to affected Enterprise customers within **24 hours** of the incident resolution.
