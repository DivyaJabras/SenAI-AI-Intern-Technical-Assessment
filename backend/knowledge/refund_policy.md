# Refund Policy

We want you to be satisfied with our service, but we also maintain strict billing guidelines to ensure fairness.

## Standard Refund Window
No refunds are issued after **14 days** from the payment date. If you cancel your subscription after 14 days, you will retain access to your account until the end of the current billing period, but you will not receive a refund for the current period.

## Exceptions
Exceptions to the standard refund policy are rare and require a written request. These requests are reviewed by our billing team and will be processed within **5 business days**.

## Retention Playbook (For Internal Use)
When encountering a customer threatening to churn or requesting an out-of-policy refund, the following escalation path must be followed:
1. **Service Credit**: First, offer a service credit of up to one free month to retain the customer.
2. **CSM Escalation**: If a credit is insufficient, offer a direct call with a Customer Success Manager to resolve their concerns.
3. **Refund Processing**: Only process a refund as an absolute last resort, provided the customer is still within the 14-day window.
