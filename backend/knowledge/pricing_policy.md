# Pricing Policy

We offer three main pricing tiers to fit businesses of all sizes:

1. **Starter Plan**: $49/month
   - Up to 5 users
   - Standard response times
   - Basic API access

2. **Standard Plan**: $149/month
   - Up to 25 users
   - Priority email support
   - Full API access

3. **Enterprise Plan**: Custom pricing
   - Unlimited users
   - Dedicated Customer Success Manager
   - 24/7 phone support and custom SLA
   - Advanced compliance features (HIPAA, Data Residency)

## Non-Profit Discount
We are proud to support organizations making a difference. Non-profit organizations with a valid 501(c)(3) status receive a **30% discount** on the Standard plan.

## Pro-Rata Billing
When upgrading your plan in the middle of a billing cycle, you will only be charged for the remaining days in the current billing cycle at the new rate. This is known as pro-rata billing.
