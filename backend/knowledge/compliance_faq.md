# Compliance and Security FAQ

## HIPAA Compliance
**Q: Do you offer a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for HIPAA compliance?**
A: Yes, we offer a BAA, but it is available on the **Enterprise plan only**. Starter and Standard plans cannot process protected health information (PHI).

## GDPR
**Q: How do you handle GDPR Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)?**
A: Customers on the **Standard and Enterprise plans** can review and sign our standard DPA directly through the online dashboard under "Security Settings."

## SOC 2
**Q: Are you SOC 2 compliant?**
A: Yes, we maintain SOC 2 Type II certification. The latest audit report is available upon request for active customers.

## Data Residency
**Q: Can we choose where our data is hosted?**
A: Yes, data residency options (including US and EU regions) are available, but strictly for customers on the **Enterprise plan**.
