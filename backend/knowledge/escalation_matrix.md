# Escalation Matrix

This document defines the routing rules for specific types of critical or sensitive emails.

## Legal and Security
- **Legal Threats & Cease-and-Desist**: Route immediately to the **Legal Team** (`legal@company.com`). Do not auto-reply or make admissions.
- **Security Incidents**: Route to the **Security Team** (`security@company.com`) with an immediate Slack notification.
- **Ransomware / Extortion**: Route to **Security + Executive Team**. **Under no circumstances should you engage with or reply to the attacker.**

## Public Relations & Churn
- **PR Crises & Public Review Threats**: Escalate to the **Customer Success Director** and the **PR Team**.
- **VIP Churn Threats**: Any account with a value over $10,000 threatening to cancel must be escalated directly to the **VP of Customer Success**.

## Data Privacy
- **GDPR / Data Subject Requests**: Route to the **Data Protection Officer** (`dpo@company.com`). The DPA mandates that we must respond to these requests within **72 hours** of receipt.
